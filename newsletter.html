If Homeowners Pay Less, Who Pays More?

By Dina Ulrich · September 29, 2026

Amendment 3 would raise Florida’s homestead exemption to $250,000 on non-school taxes by 2028. Palm Beach County estimates it could lose nearly $250 million once it’s fully in place — and because Seven Bridges sits in unincorporated Palm Beach County, the county’s choices are the ones that land on our bills. Here’s what Seven Bridges owners should know about who picks up the difference, including the December 31 residency cutoff.
